# src/lib rules

- Replacements (free resend for a delivered order, no pickup/restock) are created only via the `create_replacement_order` RPC, rules mirrored in pure `src/lib/replacementRules.ts`: the replacement is an `is_return` order tagged `replacement` linked by `original_order_id`, and the lead closes as `merged` with `merged_into_order_id` + tag `converted_to_replacement`, because merged leads are already excluded from queue, stock and cancel stats.
- Per-SKU landing benefit pills live in `src/lib/productBenefitBadges.ts` and render through the shared `ProductBenefitBadges` component below the product image, so badge copy, contrast and placement stay consistent across /p pages.
- Direct-response landings in the camera layout family are registered in `DR_LANDINGS` (`src/lib/drLandings.ts`); the camera entry mirrors its existing constants and its page is untouched, while product-id entries (e.g. SATORI) render their own themed page (SATORI computes its package rows via `satoriTiers` from product price/compare and shared tiers, no timers) through GenericLanding's shared checkout, bump and analytics.

- Wood-disc is an isolated DR_LANDINGS product-id presentation through GenericLanding; public sample reviews remain explicitly illustrative and excluded from real ratings, and its evergreen timer repeats through the pure woodDiscTimer helper without writing prices, so existing checkout ownership and truthful proof stay intact.
- Wood-disc uses its uploaded grinder clip and poster as presentation fallback when editor media slots are empty; configured media takes precedence, preserving editability without altering checkout or other landings.
